WHAT SONICHOICE DOES

Built for how delivery companies actually operate

Not a generic logistics tool. Sonichoice is designed specifically for multi-branch delivery companies that move parcels between locations.

Parcel tracking

Log every parcel with full details — sender, recipient, size, fee, route. Know exactly where it is at any point in its journey.

CORE FEATURE

Branch management

Manage all your branch locations in one place. See which parcels are sitting where and how long they've been there.

OPERATIONS

Movement history

Full timeline for every parcel. Every branch it passed through, every status change — all logged and visible to staff and admin.

TRANSPARENCY

Client profiles

Keep records of every sender. Track how many parcels each client has sent, their total spend, and all active deliveries.

CRM

Role-based access

Admins see everything. Staff see only what they need. Admin-only pages don't even appear in staff navigation.

SECURITY

Live dashboard

At-a-glance metrics for in-transit, received, pending, and revenue. Branch-by-branch breakdown and activity.

ANALYTICS

THE WORKFLOW

From drop-off to received

Client drops off

Client brings a parcel to any branch. Staff logs it with recipient details and size.

Parcel moves

Staff dispatch it to the next branch. Each movement updates the parcel's location and timeline.

Arrives at destination

When it reaches the delivery branch, staff confirm arrival. Parcel shows as ready for final delivery.

Received

Arrived at the destination branch. Marked received. Full history recorded.
